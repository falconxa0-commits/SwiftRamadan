info What is SwiftRamadan?

SwiftRamadan is an AI-powered halal food delivery platform purpose-built for Ramadan — guaranteeing delivery before Maghrib so no one breaks fast alone. Powered by Smart Kitchen AI for intelligent meal planning and cooking assistance, Community Group Buying for collective savings, and 7 AI skills spanning vision, speech, and generative capabilities, SwiftRamadan transforms the Ramadan dining experience from stressful to seamless. It's a three-sided marketplace connecting Muslim customers, halal vendors, and delivery riders — all unified by faith, food, and technology.
